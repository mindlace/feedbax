# Version lineage and what changed over time

Source: structural box/line diffs across ~28 archived `.maxpat` snapshots (2018–2025), file the consecutive-version structural diffs (generated with `tools/maxdiff.py`), plus full listings of four early snapshots (v54, v70, v83, v84). Dates below are each file's save timestamp as recorded in the archive, not necessarily the true creation date (see caveats at the end). **Sean reused version numbers across divergent branches** (there are two "v116"s, two "v117"s, two "v121"s, two "v122"s eleven months apart, each a different lineage) — treat the number as a branch label, not a sequence counter; the date is the only reliable ordering signal.

### 2. Narrative: core loop and shader-chain evolution

**Core loop.** From the earliest snapshot (v54, 2017/2019) the frame loop already has its mature shape: `metro @interval 60 hz` → `t to_texture b b b … erase` firing right-to-left → erase → bang the previous-frame texture → **shaderfx** → mirrored `jit.gl.videoplane` → `jit.gl.render` draw/swap → `to_texture` capture for next frame [the listing of archived `v54_leap2017` lines 41–89]. What changes over 8 years is not this skeleton but (a) how many named sub-buses hang off that trigger object — it grows from 6 outlets (`to_texture erase` + 4 bangs) in v54/v70 to 9 outlets by v121jremvOrigImg, adding `s ctrlbang`/`s imgbang`/`s audiobang`/`s bonusbang`/`s bang1` one at a time as new subsystems (picsVid, sound2, misc) needed their own per-frame trigger — and (b) the resolution/window model, which goes from hard-coded per-machine `dim`/`size` message boxes (v54–v83; a wall of `msg "dim 2560x1600"`-style boxes, one per known display) to an externally-driven `s/r resolution` bus (from v96 RELEAP onward) with the fullscreen/preview 320×180 split and `usetexture fst/dst` fixed early and never revisited.

**Shader chain.** **[corrected — this whole subsection was re-dated and re-sequenced against the diffs; the original four-era account misattributed the `jit.gl.pix` introduction to v84 instead of v88, and treated brcosa as continuously present v84→v122debuggingisg when it in fact has a ten-month gap where it is completely absent from the file.]** The color/geometry pipeline in `p shaderfx` actually goes through six stages:
1. **v54–v87 (2017 – Jan 17 2023): pure GLSL slab chain.** `td.rota.jxs` (rotate/zoom/pan) → `cc.modhuenew.jxs` (hue modulation) → `cc.scalebias.jxs` (brightness via scale+bias) → outlet [the listing of archived `v54_leap2017` lines 904–1004]. All three are `jit.gl.slab` fragment-shader passes; no `jit.gl.pix` (gen-graph shader) exists yet. `shaderfx` is untouched by the "+ image" v84 snapshot (no diff at all vs v83) — it stays in this state through v84.
2. **v88 (Jan 30 2023): `jit.gl.pix` arrives.** A `jit.gl.pix @gen brcosa` (brightness/contrast/saturation, driven by three `live.dial`s) and a second `jit.gl.pix` (hue_shift/hue_gain/luminance_gain/saturation_gain) are inserted; `cc.modhuenew.jxs` is disabled in place (`loadmess enable 0`) rather than deleted — it survives as dead weight in the patch from here on. Chain becomes `td.rota.jxs → cc.scalebias.jxs → jit.gl.pix(brcosa) → jit.gl.pix(hue/sat/lum) → outlet`.
3. **v105 (Jan 15 2024): scalebias and brcosa both bypassed.** `td.rota.jxs` is rewired straight to `jit.gl.pix(hue/sat/lum)`, skipping `cc.scalebias.jxs` and `jit.gl.pix(brcosa)` entirely. Both objects (and brcosa's three `live.dial`s) remain present in the patch, just disconnected — an orphaned island, not yet deleted.
4. **v108 (Feb 3 2024): the orphans are cleared out.** `cc.scalebias.jxs`'s box is repurposed into `pak param anchor 0. 0.` feeding `td.rota.jxs` (zoom/rotation now pivot around a settable anchor point instead of a separate GLSL scale+bias pass). `jit.gl.pix(brcosa)` and its three `live.dial`s are *physically deleted*. Chain: `td.rota.jxs(+anchor) → jit.gl.pix(hue/sat/lum) → outlet` — **brcosa is completely absent from the file**, not merely disconnected, for the next ten months.
5. **v121jremvOrigImg (Dec 8 2024): brcosa is rebuilt from scratch, in reversed order.** A new `jit.gl.pix @gen brcosa` object, three new `live.dial`s, and a `gswitch2`-gated manual Leap-override switch all reappear — not restored from any earlier snapshot, freshly authored. This time brcosa sits *after* the hue/sat stage: `td.rota.jxs(+anchor) → jit.gl.pix(hue/sat/lum) → jit.gl.pix(brcosa) → outlet`. This form is stable through the rest of the Max‑8 "jamian" performance branch, including Sean's last dated snapshot, v122debuggingisg (2025‑09‑20).
6. **v121DeployabilityCleanup (June 2025, Max 9 branch): brcosa disconnected again — but this time left in place.** `jit.gl.pix(hue/sat/lum)` now wires directly to the outlet; the two connections to/from brcosa are cut. Unlike the v108 cut, the `jit.gl.pix @gen brcosa` object and its three `live.dial`s are **not deleted as boxes** — no such box-removal appears in this diff, and the current repo's own `shaderfx.maxpat` confirms it: obj‑47 (`jit.gl.pix @gen brcosa`), its live.dials (obj‑121/129/142) and message boxes (`brightness $1`/`contrast $1`/`saturation $1`) are all still present and still wired to *each other*, just orphaned from the `td.rota`→outlet path [the `feedbax.shaderfx.maxpat` listing lines 71‑157]. A "Color Invert (unused atm)" comment and two orphaned `* 1.` passthroughs appear alongside it, suggesting an in-progress refactor rather than a finished redesign. **This state carries unchanged into v123**, the current repo baseline — see §4. Practically, this means a port that wants brcosa back does not need to reconstruct it from an old snapshot: the gen graph, its default UI values, and its internal wiring are sitting intact in the current file, only the two outer connections are missing.

**Control-source selection (Web / Leap / iPad).** The `shadeCtl` (webUI) vs `shadeCtlLeap` (Leap) selection mechanism appears first at v100 (Jan 2024): `r shadeCtl`/`r shadeCtlLeap` → `gswitch`, gated by `r leapOverride`. From v121jremvOrigImg (Dec 2024) it's a manual toggle in the webUI ("Use Leap Motion" / obj-45 toggle → `s leapOverrideUI`), present through every jamian-branch build up to v122debuggingisg. The DeployabilityCleanup branch removes that manual toggle entirely (v121DeployabilityCleanup) and replaces it with an automatic hand-presence detector `r leap2HandsActive` (v121DeployabilityCleanup) plus a 2-second no-hands auto-revert-to-iPad timer (v122DeployabilityCleanup, explicit comment quoted above). This redesign is deliberate and well-documented (unlike the brcosa removal), but it means **the control-arbitration UX in the current repo (auto hand-detect + 2s timeout) was never tested in a dated snapshot that also has brcosa live** — the two changes land in the same commit/session.

**Image/video input (`jit.gl.layer`).** First appears as `p vid` at v84 (Jan 2023, the "+ image" milestone), renamed `p picsVid` at v88. It grows enormously at v111usbHDMI (Mar 2024) with NDI camera receive, USB/HDMI camera source selection, and luma/chroma-key compositing — this is the point the instrument becomes able to key a live camera feed into the feedback loop, not just static/movie images. That subsystem (NDI, camera select, key/chroma controls) is never removed in any later snapshot in this set and is present, apparently unchanged, in v123.

**Leap control front-end.** Rewritten twice: `LeapInterface` (embedded directly in shaderfx, v54) → extracted to `p leapControl` (v76, "Web+Leap") → `p newLeap` (v92) → `p LeapGemini` (v96 "RELEAP", Jan 2024) — the name that survives to v123. Each rewrite is a full subpatcher replacement (box counts jump, not incrementally patched), consistent with Sean re-architecting the Leap integration from scratch each time rather than refactoring it.

**Named side-branches:**
- **`worldbump`** (v109waves/v110worldbump, Feb 2024): an audio-envelope-driven push on the main videoplane's Z position — `sound2` computes `avg~` of a gated audio signal → `s wavebumpsig`/`s worldBump`; root patcher receives `r worldBump` and feeds it into `pak position 0. 0. 0.`'s Z inlet (obj‑72:3 in that snapshot), gated on/off by a "Wordlbump" (sic) UI toggle and `s wordBumpEn`. Present continuously (unchanged box count in `sound2`, 348/341, from v116jamian through v123) — **not** dropped.
- **`waves`** (v109waves): a pair of `cycle~ 440` tone generators mixed and enveloped, feeding the same `wavebump`/`worldBump` bus pair — effectively the audio-reactive "bump" *is* the waves feature; they were introduced together and share wiring.
- **`usbHDMI`** (v111usbHDMI onward): see Image/video input above.
- **`Langton`**: files named `Feedbax gl + image v117Langton.maxpat` / `v118Langton.maxpat` / `v119Langton.maxpat` exist in the wider archive (found by filename search, dated by directory context to ~2024) but are **not included in this lineage diff set** and were not read for this section — their content (presumably a Langton's-Ant/cellular-automaton visual mode, given the name) is unknown. `[?]` Flagged as a sibling experimental branch, status and eventual fate (merged? abandoned?) undetermined from available material.
- **`CASImages`** (v122CASImages, Apr 2025): not a technical subsystem — just a sticker/theme image-asset folder swap (`StickersWithAlphaCh/CAStest/`, `.../NYC/`) for `picsVid`'s image source, alongside a `jit.brcosa`+`attrui saturation` pair that briefly existed on the image layer (separate from shaderfx's own brcosa) and was removed same-version.
- **`ULTRAWIDE`** (v121ULTRAWIDEss, Jan 2025): a second-output-window mechanism for a dual-ultrawide-monitor physical rig (`s/r UW2screen`, key‑126 toggle, 6400×1800 dims). **Abandoned one version later** (v117jamianXUIipadupgrade, Feb 2025) — all of it deleted and replaced with generic `attrui` inspector placeholders. A *different*, simpler border/position mechanism for (apparently) the same physical multi-monitor need reappears at v122CASImages (Apr 2025) and survives, with one round of removal/restoration in the v116/v120 jamianXUIipadupgrade pair, into v123.
